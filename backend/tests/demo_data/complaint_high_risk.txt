SUBJECT: Serious Product Quality Complaint - Physical Discoloration on Paracetamol 500mg Tablets

PRODUCT DETAILS:
- Product Name: Paracetamol Tablets
- Strength / Dosage Form: 500 mg, Uncoated Tablets
- Batch / Lot Number: PCM240817
- Manufacturing Date: 2024-03-15
- Expiration Date: 2026-03-14
- Affected Quantity: 12 blister packs (120 tablets) out of 100 boxes received in Shipment #SH-9821.

DESCRIPTION OF DEFECT & INCIDENT:
During routine dispensing at our inpatient ward pharmacy on September 7, 2024, staff observed pronounced brown-yellow speckles and surface discoloration across multiple tablets upon opening the Alu-PVC blister cavities. Initial spot testing revealed tablet surface friability and delayed dissolution.

PATIENT & CLINICAL IMPACT:
No patients have ingested the discolored tablets as the defect was detected during pre-dispensing verification. However, this poses severe contamination, stability failure, or subpotency concerns.

REQUESTED ACTIONS:
1. Immediate Out-Of-Specification (OOS) / Deviation investigation.
2. Retention sample inspection for Batch PCM240817.
3. Review of blister sealing integrity and environmental humidity controls during manufacturing.
4. Evaluation of health hazard and consideration of batch quarantine/recall.
